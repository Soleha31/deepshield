What does a DeepShield report contain?

Each analysis provides information that helps you understand why a conversation was considered suspicious.

🎯

Risk Score

A 0–100 score representing the overall risk detected in the conversation.

🤖

AI Probability

Shows the probability generated by the trained scam classification model.

🧠

Manipulation Tactics

Shows detected urgency, fear, authority, reward and financial pressure patterns.

🔎

Evidence

Highlights suspicious words, phrases and sentences found by AI.

📝

Transcript

Displays the conversation text used during the analysis.

🛡️

Safety Advice

Provides practical recommendations for handling suspicious calls.

🛡️

Important Safety Reminder

Never provide OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, card PINs or other confidential information to an unknown caller.
