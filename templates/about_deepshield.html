About DeepShield.

DeepShield is an AI-powered voice scam detection prototype designed to help identify suspicious calls and social engineering.

DeepShield uses AI-assisted analysis to flag potentially suspicious conversations.

What is DeepShield?

DeepShield analyzes available call recordings or transcripts to identify possible scam patterns and suspicious requests.

How it works

01

Audio transcription

Convert supported audio into text when transcription succeeds.

02

Scam pattern analysis

Analyze suspicious requests and manipulation tactics.

03

Risk explanation

Display available results, evidence and safety recommendations.

Important limitations

AI predictions may be incorrect. A low risk score does not guarantee that a call is safe. Always verify suspicious claims.
